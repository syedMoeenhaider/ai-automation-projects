## 01 — Banking Calling Agent

Alhamdulillah, project 1 of 8 is done — a Banking Calling Agent.

It calls customers about due loan EMIs and credit card payments, checks identity
with just the last 4 digits, and lets them pay, reschedule, or talk to a human —
all without a human agent dialling a single number.

Built with Vapi for voice and n8n for the workflow: due customers load from a
sheet, calls go out one by one, and every outcome is logged back automatically.

What is the first boring call your business would hand to an AI agent?

#AI #AIAgents #n8n #Vapi #Automation #Banking
